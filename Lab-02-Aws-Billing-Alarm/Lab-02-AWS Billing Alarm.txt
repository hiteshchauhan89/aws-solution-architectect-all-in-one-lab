# Lab 02 - AWS Billing Alarm

This lab demonstrates how to create an AWS Budget and configure billing alerts to monitor your AWS spending.

---

## 1. Configure an AWS Budget

### 1.1
Open the AWS Billing Console and navigate to the **Budgets** page:

https://console.aws.amazon.com/billing/home?#/budgets

### 1.2
At the top of the page, click **Create Budget**.

### 1.3
Under **Select Budget Type**, choose **Cost Budget**.

### 1.4
Click **Set up your budget**.

### 1.5
Under **Name**, enter a descriptive name for your budget, such as **Monthly Budget**.

The budget name must be unique within your AWS account and can include:

- Uppercase letters (A–Z)
- Lowercase letters (a–z)
- Spaces
- Special characters: `_ . : / = + - % @`

Under **Period**, choose how often you want the budget to reset:

- **Monthly**
- **Quarterly**
- **Annually**

### 1.6
Under **Budgeted Amount**, enter the maximum amount you want to spend during the selected budget period.

For Monthly and Quarterly budgets, enter the amount you plan to spend for each period.

---

## 2. Configure Budget Alerts

### 2.1
Click **Configure Alerts**.

### 2.2
Under **Alert 1**, choose one of the following alert types:

- **Actual** – Sends a notification based on your current AWS spending.
- **Forecasted** – Sends a notification when AWS predicts your spending will exceed the budget before the end of the billing period.

Under **Alert Threshold**, enter the value that will trigger the notification.

You can specify either:

- A **Dollar Amount**
- A **Percentage (%)** of your budget

**Example:**

If your monthly budget is **USD 200** and you want to receive a notification when spending reaches **USD 160 (80%)**, you can enter:

- **160** (Dollar Amount)
- **80** (% of Budget)

### 2.3
Next to the threshold value, choose the alert type:

- **Dollar Amount** – Sends an alert when the specified dollar amount is exceeded.
- **% of Budgeted Amount** – Sends an alert when the specified percentage of the budget is reached.

### 2.4
Under **Email Contacts**, enter the email address(es) that should receive budget notifications.

Click **Add Email Contact**.

> **Note:** You can specify up to **10 email addresses**, separated by commas.

### 2.5
Click **Confirm Budget**.

### 2.6
Review your budget configuration.

Click **Create** to create the AWS Budget and enable billing alerts.

---

## Lab Summary

In this lab, you learned how to:

- Create an AWS Cost Budget
- Configure Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual budgets
- Set spending thresholds
- Configure Actual and Forecasted alerts
- Receive email notifications when your AWS spending exceeds your budget
- Monitor AWS costs proactively using AWS Budgets
